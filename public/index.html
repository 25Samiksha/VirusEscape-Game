The outbreak has started. Your mission is simple: survive, collect coins, use power-ups, and escape every level.

🎯 YOUR MISSION
Survive for 30 seconds on each level without losing all 3 lives. Reach Level 20 and become the ultimate survivor.
🧑
Move & Survive Use ← → or A / D to move. On mobile, swipe inside the game area.
☠️
Avoid Viruses Red viruses damage your lives. Lose all 3 lives and you must retry the level.
🟡
Collect Coins Coins increase your score. Consecutive collections create a combo bonus.
⚡
Grab Power-Ups Power-ups activate shield, magnet, speed, extra life and attack abilities.
👹
Watch for the Boss Boss battles appear on Levels 5, 10, 15 and 20. Press SPACE to attack the boss.
💣
Survive Hazards Later levels introduce bombs, lasers, moving barriers and unsafe zones.
🏆
Level Up Every completed 30-second level unlocks the next level. There are 20 levels.
🎵
Game Audio Sounds play for coins, hits, bosses, power-ups and level completion.
